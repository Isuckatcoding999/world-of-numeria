🏘️ Beginner Math (Floors 1–10)

  • Counting: 1, 2, 3, …
  • Addition: 7 + 5 = 12
  • Subtraction: 10 − 3 = 7
  • Multiplication: 4 × 6 = 24
  • Division: 24 ÷ 6 = 4
  • Fractions: 3/4 + 1/4 = 1
  • Decimals: 0.75
  • Percents: 75% = 0.75
  • Perimeter: P = 2l + 2w
  • Area: A = lw

🏙️ Algebra (Floors 11–25)

  • Simplify Expressions: 3x + 4x = 7x
  • Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
  • Slope-Intercept Form: y = mx + b
  • Point of a Line: (x, y)
  • Solving Linear Equations: 2x + 3 = 11
  • Adding Polynomials: (2x² + x) + (x² − 3)
  • Subtracting Polynomials: (3x² − x) − (x² + 4)
  • Simplifying Radicals: √50 = 5√2
